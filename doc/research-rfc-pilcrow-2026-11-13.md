It is followed by `¶\n\nAll`. Before this fix sentence_ranges omitted it because the period's next character was not whitespace; validate_rfc_claim also rejected its exact period-ending witness for that reason. Independently, historical quote_ranges was empty because modern RFC body identity had been lost; the declared-body fix addresses that earlier boundary separately.
